Thanks for helping. Three things to know before you send a change.

## Sign off your commits

Every commit needs a `Signed-off-by` line, which `git commit -s` adds:

    Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>

It means you agree to the Developer Certificate of Origin
(https://developercertificate.org): you wrote the change, or otherwise have
the right to submit it under this project's license.

## Relicensing

By submitting a contribution, you also grant the project owner (GloriousBrendon)
a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free right to relicense your
contribution under other terms, including commercial licenses. You keep the
copyright in your work, and it stays available under AGPL-3.0-or-later.

The Aflos name and logo aren't covered by the code license; see the
Trademark section of the Aflos README.
