# Test audio

`quiet-voice.wav`: 10 seconds (from 0:02) of "Gettysburg by Britton.ogg",
a reading of the Gettysburg Address, from Wikimedia Commons:
<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gettysburg_by_Britton.ogg>.
Public domain (attribution requested, not required). Made with ffmpeg:
48 kHz mono 16-bit, lowered 27.7 dB to a mean of -50 dBFS (peak -32.4),
a quiet microphone's level (SPEC-audio, PLAN-audio Task 3).
